Jakub Malik, PhD

Assistant Professor | Sports Psychology, Psychophysiology & Motor Control

Poznan University of Physical Education

Researcher at the Department of Sports Psychology. My academic work focuses on psychophysiology, motor learning and control, cognitive functioning, and the interplay between physical activity and neuroplasticity. I investigate placebo effects, cognitive-motor interactions, and the impact of ergogenic substances and nutritional interventions on human performance.
